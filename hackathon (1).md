## About the Organizer — WeMakeDevs
WeMakeDevs is a developer community platform, founded by Kunal Kushwaha, that runs hackathons and meetups globally. It connects builders across 40+ countries through in-person events in 11 cities plus virtual programming, and partners with tech companies to help their tools reach engaged developer audiences.

Reported stats: 200K+ members, 40+ countries, 11 AI hubs, 100+ enterprise partners.

## Event Overview
- **Name:** First Commit — Event 01 of the "Bharat Builds Tour" (a six-event series, same structure across all stops)
- **Organizer:** WeMakeDevs, in partnership with AWS
- **Dates:** September 17–20, 2026
- **Format:** Hybrid — fully online Thursday through Sunday, with an optional in-person day on Saturday, September 19 (8 AM–8 PM) at Polaris School of Technology, Bangalore
- **Cost:** Free to register and participate
- **Mission statement:** "Helping great developer tools reach the builders who will shape the future"

## Eligibility
- Must be an Indian university student, age 18+
- Requires a WeMakeDevs account and a verified AWS Builder Center profile (verification link: bit.ly/abc-verify)
- Register once and it covers all six hackathons in the tour
- Staff and judges may participate but are not eligible to win prizes

## Team Requirements
- Team size: 1–4 members
- Each member must register individually; a team captain cannot register others
- One submission per team, and one team per person per hackathon
- Members can be from different universities or cities; teams can be hybrid (some members online, some in-person)
- Teams can be formed via the WeMakeDevs Discord before or during the event

## Project Rules
- Work must start only when the hackathon officially opens — projects begun before the event do not qualify
- Open-source libraries, frameworks, APIs, and templates are permitted
- The project must use AWS, and the demo video must show that AWS usage
- AI coding tools are allowed, but must be disclosed in the project writeup
- Any non-original work requires proper credit and licensing
- Builders retain rights to their project; WeMakeDevs/AWS may publicly display submitted work

## Technology Requirements

### Build It Track (Open Source, Local)
- No AWS account or credit card required
- Available tools: Strands Agents SDK, PartyRock, Cedar, SAM CLI + LocalStack, OpenSearch, Firecracker, Corretto

### Ship It Track (Cloud Deployment)
- Requires an AWS account
- Debit cards and RuPay accepted (a ~₹2 verification charge applies for new accounts)
- Free AWS credits: $100 per team, plus additional prize credits
- Relevant AWS services: Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3, Amazon Bedrock, Amplify Hosting, App Runner, Cognito, EventBridge, Step Functions
- Must deploy live on AWS with a working URL

## Submission Requirements
Submissions must include three components, submitted via the official hackathon form before the deadline:
1. A public code repository
2. A demo video, 3 minutes maximum, showing what the project does, who it serves, and its AWS integration points
3. A short project writeup (must disclose any AI coding tools used)

Additional notes:
- Deadlines are strict — no submissions accepted after the cutoff
- Early submission is allowed, with edits permitted until the deadline
- Judges evaluate only the submitted materials — there are no live demos
- An optional blog post can be published on the AWS Builder Center platform

## Judging Criteria
1. Idea and impact — real-world problem relevance
2. Built on AWS — mandatory use of AWS open-source tools or cloud services
3. Learning demonstrated — new skills acquired during the event
4. Execution — functionality prioritized over polish
5. Demo video quality

Notes:
- "We judge what you built, not what you spent"
- In-person and online projects are judged identically
- Cloud usage is scored only within the Ship It track
- Best UI prize is judged separately on design and usability
- The judging panel's decision is final; individual scores are not published

## Disqualification Criteria
- Copying another's work
- Misrepresenting old/pre-existing projects as new
- Repository history that doesn't match the claimed build timeline
- Harassment, cheating, or abuse of mentors — results in immediate disqualification
- On-site staff rules are binding for in-person attendees; conduct reports are handled confidentially

## Theme
"Build something that solves a real problem" — can be personal, community-based, or address an inefficient existing process, in any sector (healthcare, education, finance, etc.)

## Tracks
Two tracks, both eligible for all prizes (one submission can qualify for multiple categories):
1. **Build It** — local development using the open-source AWS stack
2. **Ship It** — cloud deployment with a live URL requirement

## Prizes
- **Ship It (First Prize):** ₹2,00,000 cash + $3,000 AWS credits
- **Build It (Second Prize):** ₹1,50,000 cash + $2,000 AWS credits
- **Best UI (Third Prize):** ₹1,00,000 cash + $1,000 AWS credits (open to either track)

## Amazon Fast-Track Interview Program
- At most 10 fast-track interviews put forward per hackathon
- Eligible only to Pre-Final Year (Class of 2028) and Final Year (Class of 2027) students
- Covers internships (6-month) and full-time roles
- Eligibility is verified against registration details and the Builder Center profile
- Winning a prize does not automatically qualify you, and not winning does not exclude you — judges nominate top projects to Amazon's University Talent Acquisition team, and screened candidates skip initial interview rounds
- Amazon reserves the right to modify or withdraw the program
- Non-transferable; applies only to named team members

## Certificates
- All participants who submit a project receive a certificate
- Winning teams receive an additional recognition certificate

## Full Code of Conduct (applies throughout the event)
Source: https://www.wemakedevs.org/coc

**Purpose & scope:** WeMakeDevs maintains a safe, inclusive, respectful, and welcoming environment for all participants. It covers all events (hackathons, meetups, workshops, webinars) and digital platforms, and binds attendees, speakers, sponsors, mentors, judges, volunteers, and organizers.

**Expected behavior:**
- Exercise consideration and respect in speech and actions
- Attempt collaboration before conflict
- Avoid demeaning or harassing conduct
- Remain aware of fellow participants
- Support constructive development practices
- Respect intellectual property and technical contributions

**Unacceptable behavior (zero-tolerance):**
- Intimidating, harassing, abusive, discriminatory, derogatory, or demeaning speech
- Offensive comments about gender, race, religion, disability, or technical proficiency
- Inappropriate nudity or sexualized language in public spaces
- Stalking, deliberate intimidation, or harassing photography
- Sustained disruption of events or streams
- Unwelcome sexual attention or contact
- Technical sabotage, plagiarism, or malicious software distribution

**Consequences of violations (may escalate):**
1. Formal warning to cease behavior
2. Event expulsion (no refund)
3. Disqualification from competitions/prizes
4. Permanent removal from WeMakeDevs spaces
5. Referral to law enforcement

**Reporting process:** Email contact@wemakedevs.org with your contact information, the incident's date/time/location, a detailed description and the people involved, supporting documentation (screenshots, links), and witness names. A response is guaranteed within 24 hours.

**Enforcement steps:** Acknowledgment within 24 hours → review by the core organizing team → action if the violation is confirmed → follow-up with the reporter on the outcome.
